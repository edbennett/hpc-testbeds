## Baskerville Tier 2 and Novel Hardware/Software testbed

The Baskerville Tier 2 system at the University of Birmingham
was funded by the EPSRC Tier 2 programme;
it has been permanently decommissioned to make way for the new Baskerville NCR system.

The Novel Hardware/Software testbed was integrated with Baskerville Tier 2,
and provided a testbed for novel accelerator architectures,
funded as part of the ExCALIBUR Hardware and Enabling Software scheme.

### Gaining access

This facility has been permanently decommissioned;
it is no longer possible to gain access.
